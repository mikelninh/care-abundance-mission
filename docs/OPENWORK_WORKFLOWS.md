# OpenWork stakeholder workflows

## Worker
1. Tap **I want to work**.
2. Choose interests, availability and location.
3. See up to three GREEN missions only.
4. Complete a 10–45 minute practical Skill Passport check if needed.
5. For regulated work, verify the legally required credential.
6. Review gross hourly wage, hours, employer and contract.
7. Sign employment contract.
8. Complete first paid task with light review.
9. Receive payroll.
10. Gain verified skill evidence and higher-level access.
11. At 120 paid hours or 12 recurring weeks, trigger regular-job review.

The worker never carries funding or reimbursement risk.

## Organisation with a need
1. Write one sentence: **What needs to get done?**
2. OpenWork converts it into units, hours, skills, safety boundaries and measurable outputs.
3. Confirm scope and quality definition.
4. OpenWork calculates fully loaded employment cost.
5. Existing organisation budget becomes source one.
6. Funding Engine searches compatible sources for the gap.
7. Supply only evidence needed for selected routes.
8. Mission stays RED/YELLOW until funding is binding and payroll liquidity is reserved.
9. GREEN mission can recruit.
10. Verify outputs through a predefined checklist.
11. Recurring need hits the job-transition gate.

## Employer / payroll partner
1. Confirm legal employment model and worker eligibility.
2. Produce exact fully loaded payroll calculation.
3. Confirm tariff/collective-agreement rules.
4. Provide employer documents required for subsidies.
5. Do not sign §16e/§16i-supported contract before required Jobcenter approval.
6. Confirm payroll reserve before GREEN.
7. Sign worker contract and run payroll/social-insurance obligations.
8. Return payment status/receipt to OpenWork.

## Jobcenter / subsidy authority
1. Receive structured employer + candidate + mission package.
2. Determine programme eligibility.
3. Approve, reject or request more information.
4. Only approval evidence can become BINDING.
5. Any subsidy basis below €20/hour creates an explicit premium-gap funding line.

## Foundation / philanthropic funder
1. Choose mission family, geography, target group or outcome.
2. See fully loaded cost and existing coverage.
3. Commit to a defined gap/outcome bundle.
4. Sign grant/commitment with payment timing.
5. If reimbursement-based, OpenWork separately secures bridge liquidity.
6. Receive outcome receipt: mission budget → payroll → verified result.

## Public/private mission buyer
1. Buy a measurable service/outcome.
2. Sign purchase order/service contract.
3. OpenWork reserves committed amount against mission.
4. Workers are paid independently of invoice timing only if bridge liquidity exists.
5. Buyer receives verified outputs.

## OpenWork funding operator
1. Calculate `FULLY_LOADED_COST`.
2. List funding sources and eligibility constraints.
3. Validate stacking/double-funding rules source by source.
4. Classify evidence: DISCOVERED → ELIGIBLE → APPLIED → APPROVED → BINDING.
5. Sum only BINDING money for coverage.
6. Calculate liquidity separately.
7. GREEN only if both reach at least 100%.
8. Re-check expiry/deadlines before hiring.
9. Archive approval/contract evidence.

## Quality verifier
1. Use output-specific checklist written before work starts.
2. Check evidence, not vibes.
3. Unsafe/out-of-scope work returns to coaching/rework.
4. Never use a quality dispute to erase legally owed wages.
5. Add verified skill evidence where appropriate.

## Critical failure cases

- **Grant application looks likely:** YELLOW, never GREEN.
- **Grant approved but reimburses after payroll:** YELLOW until bridge liquidity exists.
- **§16i basis below €20:** explicitly fund the premium gap; never silently cut wage.
- **Worker fails skill check:** retrain/retry or rematch; no permanent exclusion.
- **Organisation keeps requesting same task:** job-transition review at 120 hours or 12 recurring weeks.

## Definition of a successful first real transaction

- real unmet need;
- real commissioner;
- fully loaded cost calculation;
- binding funding evidence;
- payroll liquidity evidence;
- worker qualification/credential evidence;
- signed employment contract;
- completed paid work;
- accepted quality evidence;
- payroll receipt;
- worker and organisation feedback;
- transition decision if need recurs.
