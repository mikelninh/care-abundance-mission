# Portfolio Role — CARE

CARE is the public-impact delivery and guarantee layer.

Its core question is not merely "what might someone qualify for?" but:

> **What should happen next, what evidence is trustworthy, and did the promised value actually arrive?**

Public Service Passport can provide the citizen-controlled front door and reusable case/evidence experience. OpenProof can provide minimum-disclosure verification where appropriate. CARE owns the policy/delivery logic, life-event routing, guarantee design, funding/delivery conditions and receipt-backed outcome measurement.

CARE should not become a central identity store or silently replace competent public authorities.

Commercially, citizens remain the beneficiaries rather than the payer. Revenue should come from institutions funding better delivery, maintained evidence/rule packs, pilots, integration and assurance.
