# OpenWork source notes — verified 2026-08-26

This file separates **verified programme facts** from OpenWork's own design rules.

## Verified programme facts

### §16e / §16i SGB II

Bundesagentur für Arbeit states:

- both instruments require social-insurance employment;
- funding must be applied for and positively confirmed before the employment contract is signed;
- §16e: 75% wage subsidy in year 1 and 50% in year 2 for eligible cases;
- §16i: 100% in years 1–2, then 90%, 80%, 70% in years 3–5 for eligible cases;
- §16i can reimburse up to €3,000 for required further training, including in-company further training;
- subsidy is discretionary; there is no legal entitlement;
- for §16i, where the employer is not bound by/referencing an applicable collective agreement, the statutory minimum wage is used as subsidy calculation basis.

Primary source:
https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/finanziell/foerderung-von-langzeitleistungsbeziehenden

### Berlin complementary §16i funding

Berlin states that, for qualifying public-interest employers and subject to available budget, complementary funding can top wage funding back to 100% from year 3 and includes an expense allowance.

Primary source:
https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/beschaeftigung/foerderung/teilhabe-am-arbeitsmarkt/

### Soziale Betriebe Berlin

Berlin states that Social Enterprises employ formerly long-term unemployed people in social-insurance employment, provide workplace-near skill development, professional guidance and social-pedagogical support, and aim for transition to regular work during the project period. Berlin finances guidance/support and describes combination with §§16e/16i or other wage subsidies.

Primary source:
https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/beschaeftigung/foerderung/soziale-betriebe-2-0/

### ESF+ Berlin

Berlin's 2021–2027 ESF+ programme has about €143m EU funding and around €357m including national co-financing, with focus areas including education, skills/employment and social inclusion.

Primary sources:
https://www.berlin.de/sen/web/presse/pressemitteilungen/2022/pressemitteilung.1217624.php
https://www.berlin.de/sen/wirtschaft/gruenden-und-foerdern/europaeische-strukturfonds/esf-foerderperiode-2021-2027/

### Statutory minimum wage

BMAS states Germany's statutory minimum wage is €13.90 gross/hour from 2026-01-01 and €14.60 from 2027-01-01.

Primary source:
https://www.bmas.de/DE/Arbeit/Arbeitsrecht/Mindestlohn/Informationen-zum-Mindestlohn/informationen-zum-mindestlohn-deutsch.html

### Maßnahme beim Arbeitgeber (MAG)

BA states MAG is a practical employer-based trial, generally a few days and potentially longer, subject to prior approval. It is **not an employment relationship** and the employer does not pay remuneration during MAG; eligible participants continue their benefit where applicable. OpenWork therefore must not use MAG as a disguised source of unpaid productive labour.

Primary source:
https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/massnahme-beim-arbeitgeber-beantragen

### LOTTO-Stiftung Berlin example

LOTTO-Stiftung Berlin reports a €325,000 grant to Silbernetz for a three-year project supporting older Berliners with digital/social everyday problems.

Primary source:
https://www.lotto-stiftung-berlin.de/projekte/infotelefon-von-silbernetz-schlie%C3%9Ft-l%C3%BCcken-zur-digitalen-welt/

### Aktion Mensch inclusion-enterprise funding

The current offer states up to €300,000 and up to 90% of eligible costs for eligible inclusion enterprises / additional-income businesses, with an application phase 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-15.

Primary source:
https://www.aktion-mensch.de/foerderung/foerderangebote/anschubfoerderung-arbeit

### Solidarisches Grundeinkommen as precedent

Berlin states the pilot created 1,000 voluntary, social-insurance, fairly paid jobs in areas including schools, childcare, neighbourhoods, transport and social services. The programme is capped and is not an open source of new funding, but it is useful evidence that Berlin has operated a public-interest employment model.

Primary source:
https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/beschaeftigung/solidarisches-grundeinkommen/

## OpenWork design rules — not legal claims

The following are product choices proposed by OpenWork, not current statutory entitlements:

- €20/hour gross Fair Floor;
- GREEN mission only after 100% fully loaded cost has binding coverage and payroll liquidity;
- 120 paid hours or 12 recurring weeks triggers a job-transition review;
- Skill Passport for non-regulated tasks;
- progression bands above €20/hour;
- Pay Guarantee Fund / bridge-liquidity model.

These must be validated with employment-law, payroll, tax, subsidy and public-procurement experts before a live pilot.
