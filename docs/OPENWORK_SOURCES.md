# OpenWork source notes — verified 2026-08-26

This file separates **verified programme facts** from OpenWork design rules.

## Verified programme facts

### §16e / §16i SGB II

Bundesagentur für Arbeit states that both require social-insurance employment and funding should be positively confirmed before the employment contract. §16e provides 75% in year 1 and 50% in year 2 for eligible cases. §16i provides 100% in years 1–2 then 90/80/70% in years 3–5; required further training can be reimbursed up to €3,000, including in-company further training. The subsidies are discretionary. For §16i, where an employer is not bound by/referencing an applicable collective agreement, BA states the statutory minimum wage is used as the subsidy calculation basis.

Primary source: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/finanziell/foerderung-von-langzeitleistungsbeziehenden

### Berlin complementary §16i funding

Berlin states that qualifying public-interest employers can, subject to available budget, receive complementary funding that tops wage funding back to 100% from year 3 and includes an expense allowance.

Primary source: https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/beschaeftigung/foerderung/teilhabe-am-arbeitsmarkt/

### Soziale Betriebe Berlin

Berlin describes social-insurance employment for formerly long-term unemployed people with workplace-near skill development, professional guidance and social-pedagogical support, aiming for transition into regular employment.

Primary source: https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/beschaeftigung/foerderung/soziale-betriebe-2-0/

### ESF+ Berlin

Berlin's 2021–2027 ESF+ programme has about €143m EU funding and around €357m including national co-financing, focused on education, skills/employment and social inclusion.

Primary source: https://www.berlin.de/sen/web/presse/pressemitteilungen/2022/pressemitteilung.1217624.php

### Statutory minimum wage

BMAS states Germany's statutory minimum wage is €13.90 gross/hour from 2026-01-01 and €14.60 from 2027-01-01.

Primary source: https://www.bmas.de/DE/Arbeit/Arbeitsrecht/Mindestlohn/Informationen-zum-Mindestlohn/informationen-zum-mindestlohn-deutsch.html

### Maßnahme beim Arbeitgeber (MAG)

BA states MAG is a practical employer-based trial subject to prior approval. It is not an employment relationship and the employer does not pay wages during MAG. OpenWork must not use MAG as disguised unpaid productive work.

Primary source: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/massnahme-beim-arbeitgeber-beantragen

### LOTTO-Stiftung Berlin example

LOTTO-Stiftung Berlin reports a €325,000 grant to Silbernetz for a three-year project supporting older Berliners with digital/social everyday problems.

Primary source: https://www.lotto-stiftung-berlin.de/projekte/infotelefon-von-silbernetz-schlie%C3%9Ft-l%C3%BCcken-zur-digitalen-welt/

### Aktion Mensch inclusion-enterprise funding

The current offer states up to €300,000 and up to 90% of eligible costs for eligible inclusion enterprises / additional-income businesses, with an application phase 2026-09-01 to 2026-10-15.

Primary source: https://www.aktion-mensch.de/foerderung/foerderangebote/anschubfoerderung-arbeit

### Solidarisches Grundeinkommen precedent

Berlin states the pilot created 1,000 voluntary, social-insurance, fairly paid public-interest jobs. It is capped and not a source for new OpenWork places, but it is useful precedent for public-interest employment.

Primary source: https://www.berlin.de/sen/arbeit/beschaeftigung/solidarisches-grundeinkommen/

## OpenWork design rules — not legal claims

- €20/hour gross Fair Floor or higher applicable tariff.
- GREEN only after 100% fully loaded cost has binding coverage and payroll liquidity.
- 120 paid hours or 12 recurring weeks triggers job-transition review.
- Skill Passport for non-regulated work.
- Pay Guarantee Fund / bridge-liquidity model.

These require employment-law, payroll, tax, subsidy and procurement review before live operation.
