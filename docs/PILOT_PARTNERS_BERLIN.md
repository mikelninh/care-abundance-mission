# CARE Guarantees — Berlin pilot targets

Verified / researched: 2026-08-26.

The objective is not generic networking. Every target below has a concrete role in proving one part of the end-to-end guarantee.

# Priority 1 — Berlin-Mitte child-poverty / benefits simplification pilot

## Koordinierungsstelle für Kinder- und Familienarmutsprävention, Bezirksamt Mitte

**Why now**

Berlin-Mitte reports severe child/family poverty and has an active coordination office specifically tasked with improving the integrated district strategy. In July 2026 its new `Forum Bildung und Teilhabe Mitte` explicitly identified:

- complex application/approval procedures;
- fragmented responsibilities;
- low awareness of benefits;
- missing coordination;
- bureaucratic reimbursement barriers.

The forum explicitly reframed the problem from `what should families do better?` to `what can administration, politics and the network do better?`

**Named public contact**

Victoria Aharonov, Koordinatorin für Kinder- und Familienarmutsprävention
Bezirksamt Mitte von Berlin
Victoria.Aharonov@ba-mitte.berlin.de

**Our ask**

Run a 50–100 household **CARE Family Guarantee shadow pilot**:

1. no public money replaced;
2. calculate current-law support from consented/synthetic household facts;
3. compare with CARE protected target;
4. record missing applications / procedural friction / documents / days;
5. show one-page family receipt;
6. test correction UX with staff and families;
7. publish anonymised outcome report.

**What we provide**

- deterministic calculation prototype;
- source/version trace;
- anti-poverty gap view;
- worker-income curve;
- data-minimised intake;
- zero-application target workflow;
- evaluation design.

**What we need from partner**

- 1 subject-matter lead;
- access to 3–5 frontline advisers for workflow validation;
- lawful/consented path to a small anonymised or real shadow cohort;
- feedback on where official data/rules differ from our model;
- no production integration required for V1.

# Priority 4 — ESF+ / LSI: finance the development + employment proof

## Lokal-Sozial-Innovativ (LSI)

**Why it fits**

Berlin's ESF+ LSI instrument is explicitly designed for local social innovation addressing poverty/social exclusion and can connect social-policy goals with employment. Official programme material explicitly includes unemployed people, precariously employed people, single parents and families at risk of poverty, and names reduction of child poverty as a contribution.

LSI has supported:

- microprojects;
- development projects;
- model projects.

Berlin publishes current calls/timing and IBB handles programme implementation.

**Use for CARE**

Best first fit is **development/model funding**, not pretending ESF directly pays a universal Family Guarantee entitlement.

Funding can support:

- local pilot development;
- participant support / qualification where eligible;
- coordination and evaluation;
- OpenWork local employment experiments;
- social-innovation proof before institutional adoption.

**Action**

Monitor current call schedule and match CARE/OpenWork to the next eligible LSI call through the appropriate Bezirk/BBWA and implementing partner.

Sources:

# Priority 5 — Welfare / employer / payroll partner

## Berlin framework-funded welfare organisations

Berlin's 2026–2030 framework agreement with the major welfare associations provides over €93m/year across social/integration/health infrastructure; the Integrated Social Programme alone has >€35m/year and >100 projects in 2026.

This does **not** mean CARE has an automatic claim on that money. It does mean established welfare organisations already have:

- employment/payroll infrastructure;
- public funding experience;
- frontline family/social-work capacity;
- compliance/audit structures.

**Our ask**

Find one willing gGmbH/welfare partner to be the legal employer/payroll host for the first OpenWork mission and/or implementation partner for a Family Guarantee shadow pilot.

Potential channels:

- LIGA Berlin member organisations;
- existing Berlin family-centre operators;
- Stiftung SPI / other established social-project operators already involved in local social innovation.

# Funding stack — what finances what

Do not mix these categories.

| Need | Correct guarantee mechanism |
|---|---|
| OpenWork worker wage | binding commissioner/subsidy/funder coverage + payroll liquidity before start |
| OpenWork training/coordination | mission budget, ESF/LSI where eligible, commissioner/foundation/public funding |
| Family Guarantee shadow pilot | development/research/public-interest project funding; no family depends on this money |
| Family Guarantee cash top-up pilot | ring-fenced pilot cash covering conservative maximum enrolled liability + contingency |
| National Family Guarantee | statutory entitlement + public budget/treasury obligation; **not** a competitive grant |
| National software/integration | public procurement / administrative modernisation budget |

# The first three conversations

## Conversation A — Berlin-Mitte

**Question:**

> Would you let us shadow-test a zero-application Family Guarantee against 50–100 cases, without changing current benefits, and show where the current system leaves administrative or income gaps?

Success = pilot owner identified.

## Conversation B — employer/social operator

**Question:**

> Would you host/payroll 1–10 workers if every euro of fully loaded payroll is contractually funded and reserved before employment starts?

Success = exact payroll cost + contract requirements + willingness in principle.

## Conversation C — funder/commissioner

**Question:**

> Will you bind €X to one measurable mission / pilot cohort after the calculation and delivery safeguards are reviewed?

Success = signed commitment, not verbal enthusiasm.

# Gate to first real CARE guarantee

We do **not** call anything guaranteed until:

### Family top-up pilot

- cohort defined;
- maximum liability stress-tested;
- cash + contingency ring-fenced;
- paying entity legally able to pay;
- participant terms/privacy reviewed;
- deterministic calculation ready;
- payment/reconciliation/incident path tested.

### OpenWork

- need owner;
- worker role boundary;
- employer/payroll host;
- fully loaded cost;
- binding funding;
- payroll liquidity;
- qualification;
- contract;
- quality acceptance rule.

Only then does the UI turn GREEN.
