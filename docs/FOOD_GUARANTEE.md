# CARE Food Guarantee

> **No one should go without adequate food because money, paperwork or a delayed payment failed. For children, access must hold every day — including evenings, weekends and school holidays.**

This is a CARE policy/product proposal, not current German law.

## Why Family Guarantee alone is not enough

A monthly income floor reduces food insecurity, but it cannot by itself guarantee that food is actually available today. Payments can be delayed, cards can fail, a parent can face a sudden income shock, a child may be outside school during holidays, or a household may be unable to reach a supermarket.

Food Guarantee is therefore an **additional delivery backstop**, never a substitute for cash income.

## Guarantee stack

### 1. Cash first

Family Guarantee remains the primary mechanism. Food vouchers or meals never reduce the protected cash floor.

### 2. Child Daily Food Guarantee

For every child:

- healthy breakfast and lunch available through Kita/school where applicable;
- household coverage for evenings;
- automatic weekend and holiday coverage;
- no stigma or separate poor-child queue;
- medically necessary diets and allergies supported;
- quality anchored to evidence-based public nutrition standards such as DGE quality standards.

### 3. Same-Day Food Backstop

If food cannot be bought today, a person can activate emergency access without completing a full benefits application first.

Possible delivery routes:

- food-restricted supermarket wallet/card;
- direct grocery order;
- participating supermarket pickup;
- prepared meal provider;
- cash where it is the fastest lawful route.

Identity/eligibility reconciliation happens afterwards. Emergency food is never held hostage to the normal application queue.

### 4. Access, not merely money

If disability, illness, age, isolation or another barrier prevents shopping, the guarantee includes delivery or an equivalent accessible route.

### 5. Fail-closed public claim

CARE may claim **"food access guaranteed against financial or administrative failure"** only if all required channels are covered.

School lunch alone is not enough. A voucher that expires at 17:00 is not enough. A benefit that requires a two-week approval is not an emergency guarantee.

## Child guarantee state

**GREEN** only when:

1. daytime meals are covered where the child is in care/school;
2. evenings are covered;
3. weekends and holidays are covered;
4. same-day emergency food access exists;
5. access barriers can be handled (e.g. delivery);
6. necessary diets/allergies can be served.

Anything less is YELLOW/RED.

## Current-law starting points (verified 2026-08-26)

- Under the federal Bildung und Teilhabe package, eligible children can receive communal lunch in school/Kita without extra parental cost.
- Berlin already provides free school lunch to all pupils in grades 1–6.
- Berlin Kita care is generally contribution-free, but the standard meal contribution is €23/month unless e.g. BuT eligibility removes it.
- DGE quality standards provide evidence-based criteria for health-promoting, sustainable catering in Kitas, schools and other settings.

These are useful building blocks, but they do **not** yet constitute a 7-day, all-age, same-day food guarantee.

## Pilot design

Start in Berlin with 100 households/people:

- Family Guarantee shadow calculation;
- child meal coverage map by day/time;
- emergency food activation under 2 hours;
- optional supermarket/grocery route;
- home-delivery fallback;
- DGE-aligned quality criteria;
- payment/access receipt;
- incident and correction path.

Before enrollment, maximum pilot liability is ring-fenced. No participant depends on a future donation.

## North-star metrics

- children with an uncovered meal-access day due to money/admin failure: **0**;
- emergency food requests fulfilled same day: **100%**;
- children separated/stigmatised because of poverty status: **0**;
- cash Family Guarantee reduced because food benefit exists: **0**;
- medically required diet/access failures: **0**.

## Honesty boundary

No government or platform can literally guarantee that no human ever experiences hunger for every possible cause. CARE's enforceable claim is narrower and operational:

> **No eligible person is left without access to adequate food because they lack money, cannot navigate bureaucracy, or the normal payment system failed.**
