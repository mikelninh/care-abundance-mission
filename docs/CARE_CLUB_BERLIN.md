# CARE Club Berlin — Action Operating Model

> **Help today. Build the guarantee tomorrow. Prove what arrived.**

CARE Club Berlin is the public action layer of CARE. It is deliberately narrower than the long-term policy architecture: make it easy for a Berliner to find help, help someone else, contribute resources, or join the build **today**.

## What CARE is responsible for

CARE should not duplicate every charity, family centre or public office. Its technical role is to remove coordination friction and expose delivery gaps.

1. **One front door** — `need help / can help / can fund or build`.
2. **Verified action routing** — point to current trusted services; stale or broken routes fail visibly.
3. **Gap detection** — capture where no adequate route exists without publishing private family data.
4. **Guarantee engines** — Food, Family and OpenWork determine what must be true before CARE makes a guarantee claim.
5. **Funding router** — connect a verified gap to public, philanthropic and private funding sources.
6. **Impact ledger** — distinguish promised, committed, delivered and verified outcomes.
7. **Public proof** — show unresolved gaps as openly as successes.

## Division of labour: Michael + AI partner

### Michael / human founder

- choose the mission and non-negotiable values;
- talk to affected people and frontline organisations;
- build trust with partners and funders;
- make judgement calls where dignity, politics or lived experience matter;
- activate friends, companies and institutions;
- verify whether the product feels humane in the real world;
- never publish a family story or identifiable need without informed consent.

### AI partner

- maintain a current Berlin resource/funding registry;
- turn fragmented rules and programmes into deterministic workflows;
- detect stale links, missing coverage and funding gaps;
- draft outreach, pilot documents and funding applications;
- generate and run synthetic stress cases before real people are exposed;
- analyse anonymised pilot outcomes;
- maintain the impact ledger and flag unsupported claims;
- propose next-best actions based on need, urgency, location and available capacity;
- never make final legal entitlement, medical, child-protection or fraud decisions.

## 24-hour sprint

### Tonight — publish and activate

1. Publish the CARE Club action page.
2. Send it to 5 people with one request: **choose one action, not one like.**
3. Each person picks one contribution:
   - money;
   - 1–2 hours;
   - food/resources;
   - a skill;
   - an introduction.
4. At least one action should flow through an existing provider immediately (e.g. direct donation, volunteer inquiry, food rescue).

### Tomorrow — turn activity into a pilot

1. Contact one family/frontline organisation.
2. Ask only three questions:
   - Where do families still fall through despite existing programmes?
   - What can you not reliably provide today?
   - What would a useful 30-day technical/financial pilot remove from your workload?
3. Contact one food/logistics partner and one funding/payroll/social carrier.
4. Select one narrow, measurable gap.

## First real pilot

**10 Berlin households · 30 days · 0 unresolved food-access days due to money/admin failure.**

This is not yet a claim. It becomes a claim only after:

- a frontline partner agrees the cohort and eligibility process;
- maximum financial liability + contingency is ring-fenced before enrolment;
- emergency and weekend/holiday routes are operational;
- privacy, consent and incident handling are reviewed;
- every delivered support event has a receipt/proof state;
- no family is required to provide public testimonial content.

## Impact state machine

Every outcome is one of:

`NEED_IDENTIFIED → ROUTED → COMMITTED → DELIVERED → VERIFIED`

Only `VERIFIED` increments public CARE impact.

Examples:

- a person clicks a donation button: **not CARE impact**;
- a funder verbally offers €5k: **not committed money**;
- a signed grant/contract exists: **COMMITTED**;
- a family receives the payment: **DELIVERED**;
- payment receipt/reconciliation confirms it: **VERIFIED**.

## What to measure

### Direct human outcomes

- verified households served;
- child food-access days covered;
- same-day food requests fulfilled;
- euro value delivered;
- missed entitlements identified and successfully delivered;
- workers moved into funded employment;
- income after transition versus before.

### System outcomes

- time from need to route;
- time from need to money/food;
- application/admin minutes removed;
- route failures;
- stale programme links;
- funding gaps discovered;
- share of recurring OpenWork demand converted to jobs;
- appeals/corrections and resolution time.

## Non-negotiable safety / dignity rules

- no public map of vulnerable families or children;
- no sensitive need intake into public GitHub issues;
- no gamification that turns poverty into content;
- no AI-only denial of essential support;
- no food voucher replacing the protected cash floor;
- no CARE guarantee unless the delivery path is actually funded/available;
- acute child safety crises route to qualified 24/7 services;
- existing organisations' impact is never re-labelled as CARE impact.

## Product roadmap

### V0 — Action Router

Static, privacy-preserving, verified routes. Useful immediately.

### V0.2 — Route Health Agent

Scheduled link/programme verification and stale-route alerts.

### V0.3 — Partner Console

Anonymous aggregate needs, open gaps, capacities and funding status.

### V0.4 — Funding Match

For each verified unmet need: eligible public programmes, foundations, corporate buyers and required evidence.

### V1 — Guaranteed Pilot Ledger

Private case IDs, consent, eligibility trace, commitment proof, delivery receipt, correction flow and public anonymised aggregate dashboard.
