# CARE Institutional Shadow Pilot — Initial Offer

Commercial hypothesis to validate with the first partner.

## Offer

**One Life Event, Facts Once — 4-week shadow pilot**

Use 20–50 anonymised or synthetic/deidentified advisory cases for one life event (initially income loss) and compare CARE's deterministic evidence/routing preparation against the partner's existing human workflow.

No automated entitlement decision. No automatic submission. No money movement.

## Deliverables

- current workflow / evidence-request map;
- partner-specific Evidence Packet field map;
- source/provenance quality analysis;
- parallel routes for ALG I, KiZ, Wohngeld and safety-net check within the implemented scope;
- missing/unverified/invalid evidence report;
- false-positive / false-negative routing review against human ground truth;
- evidence-reuse measurement;
- recommendations for a governed next pilot;
- final impact + assurance report.

## Initial price hypothesis

**€7,500 fixed pilot fee** for one partner, one life-event scope and agreed case volume, excluding unusual third-party integration costs.

This is a starting commercial hypothesis, not established market pricing. The first three sales conversations should explicitly test willingness-to-pay and procurement constraints.

## Success gates

- no hidden binding decisions;
- zero missing→zero conversions;
- all unsupported cases fail visibly;
- routing reasons remain inspectable;
- partner reviewers can identify evidence provenance;
- measurable reduction in repeated evidence handling or preflight effort;
- no personal-data resale or secondary advertising use.

## Expansion only after proof

A successful shadow pilot may graduate to paid integration/operations. Failure should produce an evidence report and next-proof recommendation, not a sales claim that the pilot succeeded.
