# Sales Truth Boundary

Sales material may say:

- CARE can structure and validate a reusable evidence packet;
- implemented routes are deterministic prechecks with explicit missing facts;
- synthetic tests demonstrate engineering behavior;
- shadow pilots can compare CARE routing/preparation against human ground truth.

Sales material may not say:

- CARE determines final legal entitlement;
- every German benefit is covered;
- synthetic tests prove real-world accuracy or national impact;
- a route label means approval;
- OpenProof or cryptography makes an untrusted input true;
- a proposed CARE guarantee is current law.
