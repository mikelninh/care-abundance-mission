# Impact Thesis

CARE should make the social safety floor easier to reach and harder to fall through while preserving legal authority and proving delivery.

For the current public-service engineering slice, success means:

- one life event can reuse trustworthy facts across several relevant routes;
- missing/unverified evidence remains visible;
- people are not misled into thinking a precheck is a decision;
- repeated evidence handling falls;
- useful official action starts sooner;
- actual guarantees are only claimed when delivery can be evidenced.

Commercial success is subordinate to this impact contract.
