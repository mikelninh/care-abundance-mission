# Ethical Revenue Model — CARE

CARE exists to reduce poverty, administrative failure and preventable gaps in essential access. Its revenue model must not create incentives that conflict with that mission.

## Core rule

> **Charge institutions for better delivery. Do not charge vulnerable people for access to the floor.**

## Free public-interest layer

The following should remain free for individuals wherever feasible:

- basic rights/route discovery;
- plain-language explanations of supported public-service journeys;
- public policy assumptions and proof boundaries;
- access to open evidence/protocol documentation;
- tools required to understand whether a person should seek official help.

CARE must not take a percentage of benefits, aid, top-ups or public money received by a household.

## Legitimate payers

- municipalities and public authorities;
- benefits-advice and welfare organisations;
- foundations funding evaluated public-interest pilots;
- employers/funders using OpenWork;
- delivery organisations that benefit from reduced administrative work;
- integrators needing maintained evidence/routing/proof interfaces.

## Legitimate paid value

- deployment and integration;
- maintained/versioned source-backed rule packs;
- evidence adapters and data-quality work;
- workflow/policy configuration;
- shadow-pilot evaluation and outcome reporting;
- assurance/security/compliance support;
- hosted proof/receipt infrastructure;
- operational support and uptime;
- verified delivery administration where pricing is fixed or institution-value based.

## OpenWork-specific rule

The worker's wage and protections are not our margin pool.

CARE/OpenWork may charge an employer/funder a transparent platform/operations fee **on top of** the fully funded worker cost. Productive sample work remains paid. A mission cannot become GREEN because CARE expects later revenue to cover an unfunded wage obligation.

## Family/Food Guarantee rule

No revenue model may:

- reduce a household's guaranteed floor;
- skim a percentage of top-ups;
- make emergency food access conditional on a subscription;
- create a proprietary poor-person queue;
- sell household vulnerability data.

Pilots should be funded by institutions, philanthropy or ring-fenced programme budgets with the maximum cohort liability covered before enrolment.

## Data covenant

- no sale of household, identity, financial or eligibility data;
- no advertising based on vulnerability or benefit status;
- collect only evidence needed for the supported journey;
- proof/minimum-disclosure should reduce data exposure, not justify new collection;
- deidentified/aggregate evaluation data requires explicit governance and must not silently become a commercial behavioural profile.

## Pricing incentive test

Reject a pricing model if CARE earns more when:

- a household receives less support;
- a case takes longer or becomes more complex;
- more personal data is collected;
- a safety/authority gate is bypassed;
- a worker is paid less;
- an emergency remains unresolved.

## Preferred sequence

### Early stage

`paid institutional discovery/shadow pilot + integration + evidence report`

### Proven deployment

`annual institutional licence/support + maintained policy/evidence packs + optional verified workflow volume`

Blocked, failed or safety-stopped workflows should not count as successful billable outcomes.

North-star commercial metric:

> **Verified administrative value and better delivery outcomes created without extracting value from the people the system is meant to protect.**
