# CARE Commercial + Impact Metrics

Revenue is only healthy if it tracks better public-service delivery rather than extraction from vulnerable people.

## Institutional value

- administrative time removed from supported journeys;
- fewer repeated evidence requests;
- missing-fact capture before submission/review;
- correct route/precheck rate against human ground truth;
- time from life event to first useful official action;
- successful verified delivery receipts;
- integration/policy maintenance cost avoided.

## Human outcome counter-metrics

- no reduction in support caused by the product;
- no hidden authority decisions;
- privacy/data minimisation;
- complaint/correction rate;
- accessibility and completion by vulnerable users;
- false-negative route rate.

Revenue should be reported beside these counter-metrics, never alone.
